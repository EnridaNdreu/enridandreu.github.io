# About Me

I am a Mathematician by training, currently completing my PhD in Social Sciences for Sustainability and Wellbeing (39th cycle) at the **University of Florence**. My research bridges the gap between stochastic analysis and machine learning, applying these advanced quantitative tools to insurance and financial data.

I focus heavily on **path signatures** (rough path theory), claims reserving, survival analysis, and risk measurement (VaR/Expected Shortfall) within a Solvency II framework. My academic journey has given me an international outlook, having spent time as a visiting researcher at **TU Chemnitz** (Germany) and **Universitat Pompeu Fabra** (Spain).

Beyond research, I am actively involved in teaching as a tutor for MSc courses in Finance and Risk Management, covering areas like Quantitative Finance, Derivatives, and Computational Finance.

---

# Research Interests

* **Stochastic Analysis & Machine Learning:** Path signatures, rough paths, and gradient boosting for claims severity.
* **Insurance & Risk Modelling:** Claims reserving, survival analysis, and risk measures (VaR, Expected Shortfall).
* **Financial Econometrics:** Stochastic and spot volatility estimation, option-model calibration.

---

# Work in Progress

* *A two-stage framework for insurance claims reserving:* Signature-based forecasting of aggregate paid flows for closed claims, combined with Cox proportional hazards, gradient-boosted severity models, and VaR/ES for open-claim reserves.
* *Path signatures for financial volatility:* Augmenting signature-based models with Fourier estimates of spot volatility, with application to option-model calibration.
